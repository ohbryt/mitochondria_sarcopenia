# Outline — PPM1K–BCKDK–BCKDH sarcopenia manuscript

## Article identity

- Article type: integrative translational analysis with a secondary human
  transcriptomic target audit.
- Central claim: the axis is the leading mechanistic validation programme, not
  a validated human drug target or drug candidate.
- Novel contribution: explicit reconciliation of supportive published human
  multi-omics and mouse causality with non-replication of the individual axis
  transcripts across HMSR cohorts.

## Abstract

- Establish unmet need and distinguish BCAA provision from BCAA disposal.
- Summarize Zuo et al. human multi-omics and mouse causal evidence.
- Report the exact HMSR audit result for PPM1K, BCKDK, BCKDHA, and BCKDHB.
- Explain why p-BCKDHA Ser293 and isotope flux are better target-engagement
  measures than transcript abundance.
- Conclude with a validation-first, BT2-independent programme.

## Introduction

- Define sarcopenia by impaired strength/function rather than mass alone.
- Introduce the PPM1K–BCKDK phosphorylation switch controlling BCKDH.
- Describe the nutrient paradox: dietary BCAA can support anabolism while
  impaired intracellular disposal can be harmful.
- State the objective: test cross-cohort transcript generalizability and grade
  the translational evidence.

## Methods

- Targeted primary-literature synthesis through 2026-08-25.
- HMSR cohort structure: 135 strict samples, four subsets, two source groups.
- Cohort-specific OLS and DerSimonian–Laird random-effects meta-analysis.
- Deterministic target extraction with exact source hashes.
- Evidence grading by human directness, replication, causality, and pharmacology.

## Results

- Published human multi-omics supports pathway-level BCAA-catabolic disruption.
- Published aged and Ppm1k-deficient mouse experiments support causality and
  pharmacological proof of mechanism.
- HMSR individual-transcript results are all NOT_REPLICATED.
- Post-translational control reconciles pathway activity with transcript nulls.
- BT2 is unsuitable as a lead because of mitochondrial uncoupling and
  BCKDK-independent tryptophan displacement.
- Non-BT2 BCKDK chemistry demonstrates tractability but also scaffold-dependent
  pharmacology.

## Discussion

- Position the axis as a prioritized validation programme.
- Define the disease endotype and target product profile.
- Require genetic causality, p-BCKDHA, isotope flux, respiration, and specific
  force in primary human sarcopenic muscle.
- Preserve adequate protein nutrition; do not infer benefit from indiscriminate
  systemic BCAA lowering.
- State limitations: observational human data, two independent source groups,
  no human intervention, no validated selective drug candidate.

- The axis is more defensible than MARCHF5 for experimental prioritization.
